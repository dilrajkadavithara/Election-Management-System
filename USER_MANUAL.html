01 — Logging In

Getting access to the system

Open your browser and go to https://intelhub.live. You will see the login screen.

1
Enter your Username

Type the username given to you by your administrator.

2
Enter your Password

Type your password. Click the key icon to reveal it if needed.

3
Tick the checkbox

Click the authorization checkbox to confirm you are an authorized user.

4
Click "Initiate Uplink"

You will be taken directly to your dashboard.

⚠️

If you see "Invalid credentials", your password may be wrong or your account may not yet be set up. Contact your Super Admin to check.

💡

To change your own password, click the 🔐 icon next to your username in the top of the sidebar at any time.

05 — Upload Voter List (OCR)

Scanning printed voter rolls into the database

This tool lets you upload a scanned PDF of a printed voter roll and automatically extract all voter data into the database using AI-powered OCR (Optical Character Recognition).

⚠️

Only users with the Upload & OCR permission can access this section.

Step-by-Step Process

1
Select Location

Choose the Constituency, Local Body, and Booth that this voter roll belongs to. This is important — all voters will be assigned to this location.

2
Upload PDF

Click the upload area and select your scanned voter roll PDF. Only PDF files are supported.

3
Convert to Images

Click "Convert Pages". The system breaks the PDF into individual page images.

4
Detect Voter Boxes

Click "Detect Voter Boxes". The AI identifies each individual voter entry on each page.

5
Run OCR

Click "Run OCR". The system reads the text from every detected voter box.

6
Review & Correct

Go through the extracted data. Rows highlighted in orange or red have issues and need manual correction. Click Edit on any row to fix it.

7
Save to Database

Once happy with the data, click "Save All to Database". The voters will be added immediately.

💡

If OCR is running slowly or you need to restart, click the red Stop & Clear button. This cancels the process and frees up memory.

💡

Use Safe Mode (Direct PDF) for cleaner PDF files. Use the standard mode only for older scanned documents.

08 — User Settings

Admin only: Manage staff, locations, and party branding
⚠️

This section is only visible to users with the Constituency Admin role or higher.

The User Settings page is organized into three tabs:

Staff Accounts Tab

This is where you create and manage all login accounts for your team.

Creating a New Staff Account — Step by Step

1
Select a Role

Choose the appropriate role from the dropdown. The role controls what data this person can see. See Section 9 for a full explanation.

2

Based on the role, you will see the relevant location selector. Click on locations to assign them (highlighted in blue = assigned).

4

The new account is created immediately and appears in the Staff Members list on the right.

10 — Frequently Asked Questions

Common issues and how to solve them
I can't log in. What do I do?
Contact your Super Admin to verify your username and ask them to reset your password from the User Settings page.
The Voter Slips page shows no party options in the branding dropdown.
Go to User Settings → Party Branding tab → Click "Sync Party Data". This loads all official party logos and colors. It only needs to be done once per server.
The OCR results look incorrect or garbled.
Use the Review step to manually correct any wrong entries before saving. Click Edit on any row to fix it by hand. For very unclear scans, the AI may struggle — manual correction is normal.
I can see voters from booths I'm not supposed to manage.
Your account may have been assigned to incorrect booths. Contact your Super Admin and ask them to check and correct your booth assignments in User Settings.
How do I change my own password?
Click the 🔐 icon next to your username in the top of the sidebar. Enter your current password, then your new password, and confirm.
The dashboard is showing old data.
The dashboard refreshes automatically when you navigate to it. If data looks out of date, click on another page in the sidebar and then come back to the Dashboard.
I uploaded a voter list but now I see duplicate voters.
Contact your Super Admin. Duplicate entries can happen if the same booth is uploaded twice. The admin can remove the duplicates from the database.
A voter's phone number is wrong. How do I fix it?
Go to the Voter List, find the voter, click Edit, update the phone number, and click Save. Changes take effect immediately.
